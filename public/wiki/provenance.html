Provenance and honesty

A twin is only useful if you can tell what is measured from what is made up. Every part of this one is labeled.

Representative, not a replica. The twin is a representative food bank inspired by the Central Texas Food Bank, built from a publicly available news tour by 25 News KXXV. Neither organisation was involved in this work or has endorsed it. Cost figures are directional: public list prices and one individual AWS account; enterprise discounts and quotas may differ.

What the world model did and did not do

The world model supplied camera poses and metric depth for three zones. The areas the video never showed were not generated by the world model: they are designed footprints modeled in Blender. The generated-views stage (atlasGenerate) failed for this account and is not part of the published twin.

Source video

"Inside the NEW Central Texas Food Bank | See How Your Donation Changes Lives", 25 News KXXV, 27 July 2026, publicly available on YouTube. It tours the food bank's new Waco facility. It is credited as inspiration. No permission was sought from, and no endorsement is implied by, KXXV or the Central Texas Food Bank.
